01The problem

Manual lead handling breaks down fast.

Leads came in from a website form and an API, but nothing handled them consistently:
—Responses were slow — no one owned instant follow-up.
—Duplicate contacts piled up in HubSpot.
—Hot and cold leads got the same treatment.
—Assignment to reps was manual, and uneven.
—AI-drafted replies went out without a human check.
—Failures happened silently, with nothing catching them.
02The solution

One pipeline. Every lead, handled the same reliable way.

Now every lead — whether it comes through the website form or the API — enters one automated pipeline. It replies within seconds, checks for duplicates before creating a new contact, scores the lead by fixed rules so every lead gets the same fair read, and routes it to the right rep by tier, territory, and current workload. Everything syncs to HubSpot automatically, and any AI-drafted note waits for a person to check before it reaches anyone. If a step fails, it's logged — nothing disappears silently.
Built so a lead is never waiting, and never falls through.
03How it works

Five steps. Seconds, not hours.

1
Capture
Whether a lead comes in through the website form or the API, it lands in the same pipeline the instant it's submitted — and gets an immediate confirmation, before anything else runs.
2
Validate & dedupe
Name, a working email, and consent are checked first. Then it's matched against your existing contacts by email — a repeat lead updates that person's record instead of creating a duplicate.
3
Qualify & score
Company details are filled in, then AI reads the message to judge intent and urgency and explains its reasoning in plain language. That feeds a fixed scoring formula — the same every time — producing a 0–100 score and a hot, warm, or cold tier you can always trace back to a reason.
4
Draft & assign
AI drafts a personalized reply, but it goes to the assigned rep to review — never straight to the lead. The lead itself is assigned by tier, territory, and who currently has the lightest load, so it always lands with someone qualified and free to take it.
5
Sync & notify
Your CRM updates automatically with the score, tier, and reasoning attached, and the assigned rep gets pinged with the full picture. Every step is logged end to end, so if anything breaks, it's caught and flagged immediately — nothing fails silently.
